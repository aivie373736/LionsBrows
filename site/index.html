The complete browser, without the clutter.

LionsBrows is a fast, private browser built on Chromium. It has everything you need, stays out of your way and runs well even on older computers.

Download Source code

Free and open source · Windows, macOS and Linux

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Minimal by default

Back, forward, reload, the address bar and a menu. Downloads, split view and notes appear only when you need them.

Blocks ads and trackers

Built-in blocking, hidden cookie banners, warnings before phishing sites and private windows that forget everything when you close them.

Light on memory

Inactive tabs go to sleep and a low-memory mode turns on by itself on computers with 4 GB of RAM or less.

Reader view

One click turns a cluttered article into clean text, in light, sepia or dark.

Workspaces and split view

Keep work and personal tabs apart, even with separate logins, and view two pages side by side.

Command bar

Press Ctrl+K to find open tabs, bookmarks, history and settings, or to do a quick calculation.

Bring your bookmarks

Import bookmarks and history from Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Opera and Firefox.

Chrome extensions

Add extensions from the Chrome Web Store with one click. They sit in the toolbar and update by themselves.

Make it yours

Your own video, image or color as the new tab background, any accent color and tabs on top or on the side.
